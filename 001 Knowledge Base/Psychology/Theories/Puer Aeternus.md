## The pattern

The _puer aeternus_, or eternal youth, is someone who resists the ordinary commitments of adult life. The pattern can look impressive at first because the person speaks about big ideas and dreams rather than small talk. Over time, the gap between vision and daily life becomes frustrating.

Common signs in these notes:

- Putting life on a pedestal and feeling severely disappointed when reality does not match the vision.
- Idealizing a woman, then resenting the discovery that she is an ordinary person rather than a goddess.
- Being capable of heroic acts while remaining unable to do ordinary work.
- Building an identity around the promise, “I'm going to be exceptional.”
- Fearing the loss of potential that comes with commitment.
- Trying so hard not to waste time that time is wasted through indecision.
- Remaining like a pluripotent stem cell to avoid feeling trapped: the real life is always expected to begin later.
- Collecting possibilities without forming them into a coherent picture.
- Hunting for perfect circumstances, then blaming circumstances, other people, or a former self.
- Seeing oneself as fragile.

## A way out

- Focus on the cost of remaining uncommitted.
- Keep attention on reality rather than fantasy.
- Give up abstract potential by choosing a door and walking through it.
- Stop making excuses.
- Accept the ordinary repetition required to make progress.
