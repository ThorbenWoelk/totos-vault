## Papers and research directions

- **Attention Is All You Need (2017):** Introduced the Transformer architecture.
- **Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners:** Introduced GPT-2.
- **Language Models are Few-Shot Learners:** Introduced the 175-billion-parameter GPT-3.
- **Harnessing the Universal Geometry of Embeddings:** Explores whether representation spaces can be
translated between models.
- **Platonic Representation Hypothesis:** Proposes that different models learn compatible representations of
the same underlying reality. The source note highlights vec2vec research in which embeddings from one model
were translated so another model could reconstruct source material.
