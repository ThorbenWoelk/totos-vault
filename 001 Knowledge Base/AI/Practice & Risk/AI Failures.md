## Failure examples

Real-world AI failures show the gap between benchmark accuracy and operational reliability:

- Tesla's self-driving systems have struggled to detect pedestrians and obstacles in low-visibility
conditions.
- Walmart anti-theft systems have reportedly flagged innocent customer behavior as theft.

Large budgets, strong engineering teams, and extensive datasets do not eliminate these failure modes. Systems
still require evaluation under realistic operating conditions.
