## LLMs as reasoning partners

Current AI systems may be more useful as sophisticated argument generators than as consistently reliable
reasoning partners. The same model can produce a persuasive business idea and then produce an equally
persuasive argument against it when prompted to critique its own work.

## Prototype-quality applications

Many AI applications demonstrate technical possibility without delivering dependable value. NotebookLM, for
example, can convert a large PDF into a podcast quickly. With deeply technical material, however, the result
may sound confident and energetic while failing to teach the subject with enough precision.
