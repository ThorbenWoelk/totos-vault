## Desktop features

We have a few features that are only available on the desktop app.

Floating Chats – press Alt+C or Option+C to open a floating chat window, capture ideas, and talk to kAI
anywhere on your computer.

Floating Documents – press Alt+D or Option+D to open a floating document to write or take notes from anywhere.
You can download the Mac or Windows desktop app here.
## Core workflow

Create and organize documents in the left sidebar.

Click Chats in the left sidebar to jot down ideas like you're texting yourself. Connect those ideas to
documents or add tags for organization.

If you enable kAI inside of chats, you ask general questions, talk to your documents, talk to your sources,
and have a creative sparring partner as you write.

Click Library to upload PDFs, add sources, and save highlights. Import from Readwise to save highlights from
all over the web.
Pro-tip: If an idea comes to mind for a specific note or project, open Chats on your phone, connect to the
document with "@," and jot it down fast so you have it for later.
While you are writing, you can filter your chats to have the right ideas with you as you write.
## Navigation

Open the Search menu with ⌘+K to filter and find documents, captures, sources, elements, and tags.

Open anything in a new pane by right-clicking on a document or source and pressing "Open In Pane."

Click the dropdown under a document's title to view its properties (subdocuments, connections, tags, and
captures) in one place.
## Connections and tags and tags

Create a connection to a document, source, author, or date by typing "@" followed by the name.

Tags like #post idea or #writing help you categorize your documents and captures. Click on a tag to see
everything that contains that tag.
## Elements and reusable structure

Use Elements to create outlines, templates, and organization by typing "/" in a document and navigating to
"Elements."

Think about how you structure project outlines. Then, create and organize your elements accordingly.

For an article outline, for example, you could have elements such as a Hook, Introduction, Body, and
Conclusion.
Pro-tip: Create a project outline with elements and duplicate that outline when you need to start a new
project. Then, create a new blank document, open your outline in a pane, and start writing!
