## Instructions

KoreNotes turn an idea worth remembering into a developed idea of your own.

1. Capture the original idea.
2. Use the elements below to develop your thinking.
3. Add relevant tags and connections.
4. Remove elements that do not help.

As a rule of thumb, aim for at least three useful elements, two tags, and one connection.

## Elements

| Element | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Big Idea | What is the central idea? |
| Problem | What problem does it address? |
| Goal | What outcome should it achieve? |
| Example | Which examples or experiences make it concrete? |
| Benefit | Why is the outcome desirable? |
| Process | Which steps lead to the outcome? |
| Concept | Which new concept follows from the idea? |
