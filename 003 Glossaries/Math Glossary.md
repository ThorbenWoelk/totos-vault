## C

### Closed-form solution

A closed-form solution provides an exact answer through a finite formula rather than an iterative procedure or
numerical approximation.

## G

### Gradient descent

Gradient descent is an iterative optimization algorithm. It minimizes a function by repeatedly moving in the
direction of steepest decrease—the negative gradient. A common analogy is a ball rolling downhill toward a
local minimum.

## S

### Spatial hierarchy

A spatial hierarchy represents visual information at several levels of resolution and complexity. In a
convolutional neural network, later layers combine simple patterns learned by earlier layers into more complex
representations.

#### Biological analogy

1. Simple cells detect edges and lines.
2. Complex cells combine those signals into shapes and patterns.
3. Higher visual areas recognize objects, faces, and scenes.

#### Receptive fields

- Earlier layers have small receptive fields and capture local details.
- Later layers have larger receptive fields and capture whole objects.
- Information therefore progresses from local features toward global structure.
