## Source idea

In a conversation about mental health, spirituality, and success, Dr. K described a mind that works best by rotating among several pursuits:

> Your mind is so dynamic that you're gonna get bored easily and what you need to do is juggle as many balls as you can at the same time without dropping a single one. Your mind is designed to like do something really really passionately for a brief period of time and then switch to something else. Your best productivity is not going to come from one thing; it's going to come from rotating your attention between three or four different things.

## Personal takeaway

When I get bored with one thing, I can move to another and later return. The useful pattern is a deliberate cycle among A, B, C, and D. The unhelpful pattern is a trail of abandoned interests from A through Z.

The conversation frames people as “farmers” or “hunters.” Farmers may find satisfaction in steady routines and concentrating their energy on one pursuit. Hunters pull in disparate stimuli and connect them; their attention needs more movement. ADHD can be understood through this lens: some minds are poorly matched to environments that require sitting in one place and attending to one thing for eight hours.
