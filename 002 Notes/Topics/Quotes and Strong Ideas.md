## Diagnosis before intervention

> Good diagnosis precedes good treatment.

The PRECEDE–PROCEED model reflects this principle: understand the problem comprehensively before designing an
intervention.

## Simplicity

> Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.

This idea, attributed to Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, favors simplicity and deliberate omission over unnecessary
breadth.
